Our Story

Started because a hospital had to wait.

Manoj Sadhwani, a mechanical engineer whose family had moved to Surat from Agra, once went to a hospital needing surgical products. He was told they would take one to two business days to arrive.

Surat was a fast-growing city, but it had very few distributors for surgical products. In 1998, Manoj Sadhwani and his brother Vicky Sadhwani started Surat Surgicals together to close that gap. Through their own network they made their first tie-up and won their first authorised distribution contract with Romsons.

In 2005 the brothers started a sister company, Care Surgicals. More than two decades on, they still run Surat Surgicals as a family business, supplying hospitals across Gujarat, Daman and Diu.
